This adaptation merges available Thai values into the English dictionaries
extracted from the tested game installation. It preserves all 56,112 keys,
uses Thai in 40,520 entries, and keeps English for missing or incompatible
translations. It does not include Thai voice acting or image signs.
Coverage counts entries containing Thai; it is not a quality guarantee.
